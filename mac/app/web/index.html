How Perimeter Zero works

  1. Lay it out. Drag services from the left onto the canvas. Drop them inside a VPC, AZ or subnet and they pick up that placement automatically.
  2. Connect. Drag from the small dot on a service's right edge to another service.
  3. Tune. Select a service to set its size and usage. The monthly estimate updates instantly.
  4. Review. The Zero Trust tab rates the design on the eight CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model v2 pillars (Identity, Devices, Networks, Applications & Workloads, Data, plus Visibility & Analytics, Automation & Orchestration and Governance) from Traditional to Optimal. Each finding names the NIST SP 800-207 tenet behind it, and most have a one-click fix. The W-A tab keeps the Well-Architected review as a second opinion.
  5. Accounts. Draw account boxes (workload, security, log archive, management) around services to show where they live. Logs belong in their own account.
